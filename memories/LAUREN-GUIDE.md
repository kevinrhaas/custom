# Your family album: a short guide

Everything in the album comes from one shared Google Drive folder. Whatever you add there shows up in the album by itself, usually within a minute. You never have to touch the website.

## Adding photos

**Folders are chapters.** Make a folder for each part of the story. Put a number at the front to set the order. The number won't show in the album.

> 01 Early years
> 02 The farm
> 03 Keepsakes

Photos you put loose in the main folder, outside any chapter, go into a chapter called "The album".

**Name each photo with its year first**, then a few words:

> 1948 - Wedding day.jpg
> about 1955 - Picnic at the lake.jpg
> 1960s - The old Buick.jpg

Not sure of the year? Write "about" in front, or use the decade, like "1960s". If there's no year at all, that's fine too.

**Tell the story in the Description box.** In Google Drive, click a photo, open its details (the little **i** button) and type in **Description**. That becomes the caption under the photo. To say where it was taken, add a line like:

> Place: St. Mary's, Joliet

**Keepsakes and letters.** For a scanned keepsake (a recipe card, a medal, a ticket stub), type **#keepsake** anywhere in its description. For a letter, type **#letter**. Or put them in a folder with "Keepsakes" in its name, and everything inside counts.

A few tips:
- Scan as **JPG** (not PDF) so the album can show them.
- Bigger scans are fine. The album makes small copies for viewing, and relatives can still save the full-size original.
- To fix a name or caption, just change it in Drive. The album updates on its own.

## Inviting family

Go to **custom.polecat.live/memories/admin/** on your phone or computer.

1. **The first time only:** paste the admin key Kevin sent you and tap **Unlock**. Keep that key private, because it can make links to the album. Your device remembers it.
2. **Make a link:** type who it's for (like "Amber"), choose how long it should work (**Always** is fine for family), and tap **Make the link**.
3. **Send it:** tap **Text it** or **Email it**. A friendly message is already written for you. Or tap **Copy link** and paste it anywhere. If you're sitting together, they can point their phone camera at the square code on your screen.

Every link you make is listed under **Links you've made**, so you can copy one again later. To turn a link off for everyone, send Kevin the ID shown next to it.

## How relatives use it

They tap the link you sent, and the album opens. That's all. Their phone or computer remembers them, so next time they can just go to **custom.polecat.live/memories/**. If they use a second device, they tap the same link there too.

If someone sees "This album is just for family", they haven't opened their link on that device yet. Send it again, or have them paste it into the box on that screen.

Inside the album they can look through each chapter, see the photos by year, search for a name or place, and tap **Save a copy** on any photo they want to keep.
