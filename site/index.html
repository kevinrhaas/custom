04 · Method

Fewer guesses. More receipts.

01

Sourced, not invented

Every building, candidate and band traces back to a record. Where the record runs out, the gap stays on screen — confidence is part of the interface, not a footnote.

02

Built for a real day

Each tool answers one question someone actually had: a walk, a vote, a trip, a gift. They load fast, work on a phone, and ask for no account.

03

Made in the open

Objects come from parametric scripts, not one-off meshes, so they can be resized and reprinted. Code, data and CAD source all live in public repos.

Made to be used.

Free, open, and no account. Pick something and go.
