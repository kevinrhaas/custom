The clock trap

Nevada and Utah are one hour apart in September

Leaving Las Vegas at 7:30 AM PDT and driving 2 hours 45 minutes reaches Springdale around 11:15 AM MDT. On flight day, 7:45 AM MDT from St. George becomes roughly 8:45 AM PDT at LAS after a two-hour drive.
