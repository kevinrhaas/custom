Every building is clickable. The tint and the citation come from the same record, so they cannot drift apart. Dimensional uncertainty is separate: a building can be well documented in character and still have a guessed size, and the popup says so.

What we made up

The chips above cover attributes. These are the decisions that belong to no single attribute — what is left out, what is simplified, and what was drawn where nothing attests it.
