Notes. Prices, pot sizes, and stock reflect what Lurvey’s website showed when this guide was built and will change — always confirm current availability on the product page (each name and photo links to the site). Mature sizes and spacing are typical values for well-grown plants in the Upper Midwest; give plants their full spread if you want them to fill in without crowding, or plant ~25% closer for a faster solid look. Nearly all hostas prefer part to full shade, moist well-drained soil, and appreciate protection from hot afternoon sun (gold-leaved types take the most sun; blues keep their color best in more shade).
