## Method

Every member was checked against the repository Chicago Democrat/resident-research corpus, Chicago directories and early-Chicago histories, nearby county/local histories, institutional/archival sources, and exact-name plus justified OCR/spelling-variant web searches. Same-name hits without date/place/occupation/kinship discriminators remain candidates or documented negatives rather than becoming resident facts.
