GENERATED by `chicago/4d/tools/export_resident_audit.py --build`. Hand-edit and
`--check` says so — it runs in `tools/check.sh` and rebuilds this package from

under **T-0512**: one row per person in the town's residents layer, saying which
research ticket looked at them, what it concluded, which source ids stand behind

## Reading the table

- `source_ids` is every source id behind the person, from the card's own
  `sources`, the research row, the five evidence blocks the consolidation wrote,
  the `later_census` bridge, the occupation block, and — for a head — the
  household's own graded blocks. `src_<category>` splits the same list.
- `flag_*` columns are the unresolved list. They are not failures; they are what
  is still open, and they are what T-0517's re-run will be measured against.
- `research_ticket` empty and `flag_no_research_row` true means no cohort has
  reached this person yet — see T-0508 to T-0510 and the cohorts after them.

## Baseline

This package is the **baseline**, taken on `dev` as it stood when T-0512 ran.
T-0517 re-runs the export after the update tickets land, so the two audits
bracket the programme and the diff is the answer to what the passes changed.
