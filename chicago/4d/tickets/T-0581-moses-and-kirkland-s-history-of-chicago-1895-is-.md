Moses and Kirkland's History of Chicago (1895) is the largest Chicago work the Newberry index points at that this project does not hold: read its Chicago and Cook County families for 1835 residents, households and businesses

Moses and Kirkland, *History of Chicago, Illinois* (Chicago: Munsell, 1895), 2 vols.

**Found by T-0570** (the Newberry genealogical index, volume 1). Of the 581 Chicago and
Cook County cards in that volume, **76 cite Moses and Kirkland** and 16 of those stand on
a surname this project already holds — in the residents, the 1833-1835 poll and tax
lists, or the 1840 census. It is the largest Chicago work the index points at that this
project does not have a source record for. Andreas, which it points at more often, is
already held.

Both volumes are on the Internet Archive: `historyofchicago01mose`,
`historyofchicagov2mose`. Read `data/research/newberry_index/follow_up.json` and
`leads.json` for the surnames to look up first.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
- A source record for the work, with the IA identifier as its locator, its tier and its
  rights stated.
- The surnames `leads.json` names in Moses and Kirkland looked up in the work itself, and
  what the work says about each written into the `books` domain in the T-0492 claims
  shape, with verbatim quotes the gate rebuilds from committed text.
- Anything the work DATES and PLACES in Chicago on or before 1835-07-01 offered to the
  residents, households or business layers under the ratified ladder — in that layer's
  own PR, never off the index.
- What the work does not carry, said as plainly as what it does.
