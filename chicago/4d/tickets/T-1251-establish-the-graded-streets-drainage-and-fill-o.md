**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

1. The graded streets, drainage and fill of the South Side at the 1880s scene date,
   established from engineering records and fire-insurance mapping and written as a zone table
   in the pattern of `e1834_harbor_cut/terrain_spec.json`: every elevation citing a numbered
   zone in a research doc, every value carrying its confidence.
2. The 1856 grade raise and the post-fire fill are stated as what they did to THIS ground, not
   as general history: where the 1880s surface stands above the 1835 one, by how much, and on
   what evidence.
3. Nothing is back-projected from the modern surface and nothing is carried forward from the
   1835 spec without saying so.
