Audit the post-synthesis resident/household corpus produced by T-0487 through T-0489 and publish a durable summary of what Chicago circa 1835 now looks like in the evidence layer.

The audit must reconcile canonical household files, the resident index, published mirror, twelve resident-research cohorts, newspaper/letter-list evidence, and census crosswalk/research. Report both what is known and what remains uncertain rather than turning later census evidence or negative searches into 1835 facts.

**Acceptance:**
- Canonical and published resident indexes/household records agree and validation passes, or any inherited/environmental failure is named precisely.
- Counts are reported for households, resident persons, attested, inferred, projected residents, letter-list-only, research-corroborated, 1840-census-linked, and removed reconstructed persons/households/structures.
- The summary describes household-size/profile evidence available from the census while clearly separating 1840 observations from 1835 assertions.
- Newly promoted occupations, arrival chronologies, kinship/marital/children facts and business links are summarized with source provenance.
- Unmatched or ambiguous census heads/candidate identities remain in an explicit follow-up section rather than being silently dropped or promoted.
- A machine-readable synthesis ledger and a human-readable research summary are committed.
- Ticket/status/queue bookkeeping is reconciled when the sequence is complete, and the completion PR targets `dev`.
