**Found by T-0581**, and it is a hole that ticket left open on purpose.

Moses and Kirkland volume 1 carries forty-seven signed biographies. Exactly three are of men who
were in Chicago before 1836: **Philip F. W. Peck** (printed page 569), **Philo Carpenter** (572)
and **John Dean Caton** (575). T-0581 read the first two in full and left Caton, because the run
was one run and Peck and Carpenter are the two whose BUILDINGS this project models.

**Why Caton is worth the leaf.** `caton` is one of the 66 Newberry lead surnames citing this work.
He arrived in 1833 (`bk_mk1_006`), was the town's first corporation attorney from 4 December 1833
(`bk_mk1_011`), and this project holds him. His sketch is three leaves from ones already read, in a
text that is already committed and already page-indexed — so the cost is the reading and nothing
else.
