Moses and Kirkland volume 2 (historyofchicagov2mose) is unfetched and unread, and the Newberry cards do not say which volume they cite

**Found by T-0581.**

The Newberry genealogical index (T-0570) cites "Moses and Kirkland" on 76 of its 581 Chicago and
Cook County cards, and **the cards do not say which of the two volumes**. T-0581 fetched, committed
and read volume 1 — `historyofchicago01mose_202609`, the owner's own Internet Archive upload — and
closed half the question. Volume 2 is the other half.

**Where it is.** The pre-existing Internet Archive item `historyofchicagov2mose`. It has NOT been
uploaded to the project's own account, so unlike volume 1 there is no deposit pointer for it in
`chicago/reference/` yet.

**What volume 1 suggests about it, which is not much.** Volume 1 carries the whole 1830-1837
narrative and the biographical section runs from printed page 569 to the end of the volume, so
volume 2 is probably Chicago after about 1857 plus more biographies. If that is right, its yield
for 1835 will be the biographical section alone, and T-0581's finding will hold there too: of the
66 lead surnames, volume 1 carried a biography for eleven and only three of those men were in
Chicago before 1836.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)
- The volume's hOCR search text and hOCR page index committed the way volume 1's are
  (`data/research/books/text/`, `chicago/reference/`, a `corpus.json` entry, an exact page index),
  or a written finding that the item emits neither and what was committed instead.
- Its own contents list of biographies read, and the lead surnames it carries named.
- Anything the volume DATES and PLACES in Chicago on or before 1835-07-01 offered to the residents,
  households or business layers under the ratified ladder — in that layer's own PR.
- What it does not carry, said as plainly as what it does.
