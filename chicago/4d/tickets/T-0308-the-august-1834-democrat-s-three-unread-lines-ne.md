The August 1834 Democrat's three unread lines need the page images, and one of them is a storefront's address.



Three lines in the four August 1834 issues of the *Chicago Democrat* plainly carry a fact and
cannot be read from the transcription. T-0290 read the month and left all three inside
`[uncertain: …]` rather than guessing; each needs the page images (or a second transcription
pass) to close, and one of them is worth more than the other two together.

1. **The book store's address.** `chicago_democrat_1834_08_27`, page 3 column 3, line 2849:
   `lue store is in brontese and homeo havel`. The Chicago Wholesale and Retail Book &
   Stationery Store announces its opening on 26 August 1834 and that line is where it says
   where it is. It is the only documented storefront of the month whose placement is `none`
   for want of one legible line, and T-0263 is the ticket that would place it.

2. **Which end of John H. Kinzie's warehouse.** `chicago_democrat_1834_08_06` line 2867 reads
   `EAst`, `chicago_democrat_1834_08_20` line 3175 reads `KAst`, and
   `chicago_democrat_1834_08_13` line 2888 reads `rest` — two for east, one for west, in the
   same standing advertisement. T-0290 read `[E]ast` on the two-of-three and recorded the
   disagreement; a scan settles it. The same advertisement in the 08-13 printing also says
   the warehouse is **opposite to** something read `[uncertain: Ft. Dearborn]`, which if it
   holds moves the building off the reservation it is otherwise placed on.

3. **The Hennepin letter list, whole.** `chicago_democrat_1834_08_06` page 4 carries the
   1 July 1834 Hennepin post-office list, cut into two segmenter columns; eleven names
   survive here and nine at 1834-07-30, overlapping in one. Neither printing carries it
   whole and the printed length is therefore unknown. It mints no Chicago residents
   (ruling 1 is not engaged for another town's list), so this is the least urgent of the
   three — but it is the one that would let the coverage note state a count instead of a
   floor.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — one demonstration, never weakened to pass)

Each of the three is either read off the page images and the claim updated to
`reading: scan_verified`, or reported as unreadable at the image too and the ticket closed
saying so. The book store's placement is the one that has to move for this to be worth a run.
