Sample the eave from the part of its band the ridge band can be reached from.

Piece 1 of 5 of **T-0148 — The A1 stable cannot reach its ridge band at any pitch its family allows**, split because the parent needed more than one run's demonstration to be done. The parent keeps the full ask and its links; this ticket owns one slice of it.

**Acceptance:** the eave is drawn from the part of its authored band from which SOME pitch
inside the authored pitch band lands the ridge inside the authored ridge band, in the sampler
every generator shares; where the two do not overlap the authored band still wins and the
residual stays the gate's to report. The A1 stables dealt by the generators that use the
sampler are inside their ridge band, the ratchet records the repair, and both gates are green.

**The finding this piece rests on.** Nothing in the specification is wrong and none of the
four claims has to give way. `ridge = eave + run x tan(pitch)` is increasing in the eave as
well as the pitch, so a family's `ridge_ft` rules out part of its own `eave_ft` at a given
run — and A1 is the case that shows it: 9-12 ft eaves, 7:12-10:12 pitches, a 17-24 ft ridge,
and `outbuilding` running its gable down the long axis so the roof climbs half the short one.
Every A1 standing was sampled at 9.0-9.4 ft, the bottom of its own eave band, where no pitch
A1 allows can reach 17 ft. At 11-12 ft — the same authored band — it can. T-0145 constrained
the pitch by the ridge band and left the eave unconstrained; this is the other half of it.
