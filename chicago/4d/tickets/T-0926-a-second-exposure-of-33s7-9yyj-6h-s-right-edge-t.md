**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

- Whether a second exposure of `33S7-9YYJ-6H` exists is ANSWERED, not assumed: the FamilySearch film
  the deposit was taken from, NARA M704 roll 57, and any state or county printing of the same leaf are
  each checked and the answer written down with what was searched.
- If one exists it is deposited under `chicago/reference/census1840/` beside the exposure already held,
  under a name that says it is a second exposure, and the two are compared at the right edge —
  x3534-3602 of the held exposure, the No. of Scholars footing cell.
- If none exists, that is the finding and it is recorded on the page, so no later run spends the
  same search again.

could not rule out is a nought whose right limb falls on the printed rule at x3602, where this leaf is
curling into the binding — so the evidence that would decide it is the part of the leaf the exposure
lost. One more exposure of that edge decides the figure; nothing available on this one does.
