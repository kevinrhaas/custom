T-0306 stood `john_holbrook_store` on the South Water Street frontage of
`blk_south_water_dearborn`, one door east of Dearborn, on the Chicago American
of 1835-06-13 (Vol. I No. 2, page 3 column 6, lines 319-323; claim
`chicago_american_1835_06_13#c012`). The transcription cuts the street word to
`ack Water wirert` and the extraction reads it as SOUTH Water, with its own note
asking for a page-image pass **before anything is built**. It is built anyway,
on the owner's standing instruction that an absent building is invisible while a
conjectural one is legible and correctable — and this ticket is the other half
of that bargain.

**What the images have to settle, in order of how much they move:**

1. **Which Water street.** South or North. If North, the building belongs about
   130 m north across the river and the record moves whole — a different block,
   a different frontage, a different bearing.
2. **Which side of Dearborn.** The record adopts east, for the reason
   `chicago_american_office`'s own record adopts it (west of the crossing the
   corner lot is Lot 1 and Andreas puts Madore Beaubien's log house on it). The
   card names no quadrant. About 100 m if it is wrong.
3. **Anything else in the torn half of the card** — a house name, a second
   landmark, a dimension. The transcription loses roughly a line and a half.

Same run of images as T-0305, which wants the American's page scans for four
other readings; do them together if the deposit arrives at once.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

- The page image of 1835-06-13 page 3 column 6 is read at the resolution the
  Democrat scan pass used, and the street word is quoted as printed.
- `john_holbrook_store`'s `position.confidence` and its first guard are rewritten
  to whatever the image says — including moving the building, if that is the
  answer.
- The claim's `reading` upgrades from `transcription_mediated` under the owner's
  ruling 2 if the scan confirms it, and `docs/LIBERTIES.md` L205's guards shrink
  to match.
