FOUND BY T-0885 while counting the parcels behind the auction's rows. `heacock_house_monroe`
is the only committed structure in section 16. It stands **7.2 m outside block 118** and

rule it never could: a block held several buyers and a block placement may not name the
ground under a roof. That rule is right and it is not what this ticket asks about.

What it asks about is the AGREEMENT. The register sells **Russel E Heacock lot 7 of block
117**, one block west, and seven other parcels in the section (blocks 9, 20, 23, 25, 30,
61, 129) — none of them 118 or 95. So either the house's committed position, or the plat
grid it is read against, is out by more than L219 admits; or the house at Monroe has
nothing to do with the man's purchases and the coincidence of name is just that.

All three are answerable from committed material: the position's own provenance, the
grid's residuals (T-0797 measured a 3 % north-south overrun on the sheet, along the axis
the manuscript was torn and backed), and whether any source places Heacock's house on
Monroe Street at all.
