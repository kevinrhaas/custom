The priest keeps the register that is rung G2c and his own card cites only Andreas: st_cyr_john_mary is graded attested on a single later volume.

**Acceptance:** (state it before working — the definition of done, never weakened to pass)

**Found while landing T-0724, 2026-09-06.** With N1 in place `st_cyr_john_mary` is finally
visible to the ladder — and what the ladder says is G5, on `town_layer` alone. His card is
graded `attested` and cites `andreas_1884_v1`, a volume of 1884.

The oddity is that the St Cyr parish register — `st_cyr_register_ichr_v4`, the rung G2c that
grades 35 of this town's people — is the priest's OWN book, and it names him on nearly every
entry as the celebrant. It has never been read as evidence for him. Whether a register's
celebrant is attested BY his register is a real question and not obviously yes: the rung is
about a body of record naming a person in the town, and the man who wrote it names himself.

**Acceptance:** a ruling, written down, on whether the celebrant of a register is attested by
it; if he is, the appearances that carry it reach his identity and his rung is re-derived
with the movement reported; if he is not, the card says why the man who kept the G2c source
cannot be graded by it.
