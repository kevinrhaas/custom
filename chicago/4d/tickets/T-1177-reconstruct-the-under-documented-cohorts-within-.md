The owner: *"perhaps former slaves or other less documented individuals."* The layer names no
free Black resident; the only trace is Caton's 1833 defence of "six or seven free coloured men"
before the county commissioners (`hh_caton_john_dean.json`) and the 1840 count of 53 free
coloured persons. The grading policy notes "eighty-five of the register's Chicago adults reach
no surname anywhere else in the layer: the French, Métis, Irish and German Catholic town the poll
books never recorded." Stage `underdocumented` of T-1167.

**Rules, per cohort:**

- **Free Black residents:** a bracket — low = the six or seven of 1833 (as households, trades
  from the period's usual: barber, cook, whitewasher, labourer, laundress; the barber is already
  argued), high = a share of 1840's 53 scaled to 1835; reconstructed persons with `basis` naming
  both bounds; names from a pool built for the purpose from attested Illinois free Black naming
  of the decade (cite the source added, or state the pool is the general one) — never a
  caricature; `origin` from the arrival model's routes for the cohort (Kentucky/Virginia/Ohio
  free-born or manumitted, stated as drawn); "former slaves" only as `reconstructed` status with
  the bracket's basis.
- **Irish and German Catholic households:** the St Mary's and St Cyr rows at Chicago in window
  that T-1172 re-admitted are the core; the model's community shares fill the remainder
  among labourers, teamsters and the harbour gang (T-1173) — no separate mint here, only the
  check that the shares came out.
- **French-Canadian / Métis households:** attested ones only (Beaubien, Robinson, Caldwell,
  Ouilmette and the others the sources name), `review_required` where Métis; NO reconstructed
  member, no reconstructed household, the tool refuses it and the self-test proves it. The
  ticket states this is the standing constraint and not a research gap.
- **Native presence:** none reconstructed, none depicted (L1); attested households unchanged.

**Acceptance:** the free Black bracket filled at its low end unless a new source raises it;
the community shares table printed against the model; the refusal self-tests; LIBERTIES entry
naming the bracket and its bounds; visible in the People view with the basis on each card.

**Stop condition:** every under-documented cohort the owner named is present to its evidence
bracket, and the one the constraint protects is untouched.
