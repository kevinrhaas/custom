WHAT THE ROWS PROVE. Page 1's row carries the SAME six corrections, in the same six cells, as the
totals row of leaf n576. Page 2's row is the totals row of n578. Page 3's is the totals row of
n580. Page 5's is the totals row of n584. The division is therefore n576, n578, n580, n582, n584
(printed pages 299-303) and this leaf; page 4 is n582 by elimination and by its family count of 28,
which is exactly the number of heads read on it.

THE DISCREPANCY, stated and not resolved. The family column as read sums to 247, not to the 199
written under it. The five leaves as read carry 55 + 56 + 39 + 28 + 22 = 200 heads of family. Two
of the recapitulation's five family cells therefore disagree with the leaves they total: page 1
(53 written, 55 read on n576) and page 3 (88 written, 39 read on n580 by T-0498). A re-count of
n576 and n580 against this column, cell by cell, is filed as its own ticket; it is NOT settled by
preferring whichever figure is convenient, and no record in this domain is graded on it.
