The rainy, gusty weather that comes with the equinox caught us again before we made the mouth of Green Bay, and it stayed with us right through the night we arrived on the flats, about three miles below the settlement. There the little steamer went aground, fast and hard. Almost everybody preferred taking his chances with the weather to sitting cooped up any longer in the quarters we had lived in for a week, so we decided to trust ourselves to the small boat in spite of the wind and the rain and the dark, and in good time we made the shore.
