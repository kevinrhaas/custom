The column came out of the fort and the band began to play. The Dead March. They came on in proper order, ranks correct, and not one face among them was anything but grave. Captain Wells was in front, leading his handful of Miamis. Before leaving the garrison he had blackened his face, a token of the fate coming for him. Their route ran along the lake shore. Where the beach met a low range of sand-hills standing between the water and the prairie, the Pottowattamie escort, about five hundred of them, did not keep to the sand. They held the prairie above, and the Americans and the Miamis went on alone below.
