The weather that belongs to the equinox found us again before we had made the mouth of Green Bay — rain, and wind coming in gusts — and it stayed with us until the night we came onto the flats, some three miles short of the settlement. That was where the little steamer stuck, "fast and hard." A week in those quarters had been enough for nearly everyone; the storm looked better than another hour cooped up inside. So we put ourselves into the little boat and went, wind and rain and blackness and all, and in due time we came ashore.
