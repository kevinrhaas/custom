The rainy, gusty weather so common to the equinoctial season caught us again before we reached the mouth of Green Bay, and it stayed with us until the night we arrived on the flats, about three miles below the settlement. There the little steamer grounded, fast and hard. Almost everyone aboard preferred braving the elements to staying cooped up in the quarters we had occupied for the past week, so we decided to trust ourselves to the little boat in spite of the wind, and the rain, and the darkness. In due time we reached the shore.
